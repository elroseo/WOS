## Review rhythm

1. Process [[Task Inbox]] and other notes in `00 Inbox`.
2. Review active outcomes in `10 Projects`.
3. Check responsibilities that need attention in `20 Areas`.
4. Move completed or inactive material to `40 Archives`.

- **QMD** finds relevant notes by keywords or meaning.
- **Graphify** maps relationships across notes and supporting documents.
- **Obsidian skills** help agents create valid Markdown, Bases, and Canvases.
